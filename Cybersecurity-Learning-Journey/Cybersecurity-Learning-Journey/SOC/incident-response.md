# Incident Response Fundamentals

## What is incident response?

Incident response is the organised process used to prepare for, identify, manage, and learn from security incidents. The exact process depends on the organisation and its response plan.

## Common phases

A widely used incident-response lifecycle includes the following activities:

### 1. Preparation

Prepare people, procedures, communication channels, and technical resources before an incident occurs.

### 2. Detection and analysis

Identify a possible incident, review available evidence, assess its scope, and decide what actions are appropriate.

### 3. Containment

Limit the impact and prevent the situation from spreading. Actions should follow the organisation's procedures and approval requirements.

### 4. Eradication

Address the cause of the incident and remove the threat from affected systems, where appropriate.

### 5. Recovery

Restore affected systems and services safely, then monitor them for signs that the issue has returned.

### 6. Lessons learned

Document what happened, what worked, what did not work, and what should be improved.

## Why documentation matters

Incident notes should be clear, factual, and time-aware. They should distinguish observed evidence from assumptions and record actions taken by authorised responders.
